# Apps

Apps I've built. Each is an installable web app (PWA) that runs in the browser and works offline.

| Date | App | Core Tech | Description |
|------|-----|-----------|-------------|
| 2026 Oct | [Assembly Lab](https://snowch.github.io/assembly-lab/) | PWA/React | An interactive course exploring a processor's instruction set one instruction at a time, with the corresponding C alongside it. Lessons and exercises work offline, with progress saved on the device |
| 2026 Oct | [Programmer Calculator](https://snowch.github.io/programmer-calculator/) | PWA/JavaScript | Hex, decimal, octal and binary side by side, bitwise operations, shifts and rotates, CPU-style C V Z N flags, Unicode lookup, and 110 digital-design exercises with progress saved on the device. Ships with the interactive [Bit Lab](https://snowch.github.io/programmer-calculator/docs/bit-lab.html) and a [two's complement tutorial](https://snowch.github.io/programmer-calculator/docs/twos-complement.pdf) |
| 2026 Aug | [Yoga Nidra](https://snowch.github.io/nidra-app/) | PWA/JavaScript, Kokoro TTS | A drip-released audio programme that teaches you to perform yoga nidra on yourself, from memory and unaided — grounded in the source texts (Upaniṣads, Yoga Sūtras, tantric tradition) rather than a modern protocol |
| 2026 Aug | [Autogenic Training](https://snowch.github.io/at-app/) | PWA/JavaScript, Kokoro TTS | The six standard autogenic exercises, released one at a time, with progression criteria and a session builder, until you can reach deep calm on command |

*Yoga Nidra and Autogenic Training are self-directed relaxation practices: only practise where it is safe to relax and fall asleep, and neither is a substitute for sleep, medical, or psychological care.*
